About Big Screen Bible

Scripture, Without the Distractions

Big Screen Bible exists to make God's Word easy to read, easy to share, and easy to keep at the center of every gathering.

Whether you're leading worship, teaching a Bible class, hosting a small group, sharing Scripture online, or spending time in personal devotion, Big Screen Bible is designed to keep attention on the Bible rather than the technology behind it.

In a world filled with notifications, advertisements, and endless distractions, sometimes the most powerful thing is simply seeing God's Word clearly.

The person behind the project

Meet the Creator

Hello! My name is Dane.

I'm a Christian educator, worship leader, and lifelong student of the Bible. For many years, I've served in classrooms, churches, and ministry settings where Scripture is regularly read, taught, studied, and displayed.

Throughout those experiences, I often found myself searching for a better way to present God's Word, one that was simple, readable, and designed specifically to keep Scripture front and center.

Big Screen Bible grew out of that desire and became a project dedicated to helping churches, schools, families, small groups, and individual believers engage more deeply with God's Word.

Why Big Screen Bible exists

The Vision

To help people read, study, learn, share, and present Scripture in a way that keeps the focus on God's Word.

The platform was designed to work beautifully on everything from phones and tablets to classroom displays, televisions, projectors, and church sanctuary screens.

Whether you're reading alone, studying with a group, teaching a class, or presenting Scripture to a congregation, the goal remains the same: make God's Word clear, accessible, and central.

Clean reading, study tools, display mode, and games

What Makes Big Screen Bible Different?

Big Screen Bible is built around the idea that engaging with Scripture should be both simple and meaningful.

  • Clean, distraction-free Bible reading
  • Full-screen display modes for worship services and presentations
  • Powerful Bible study and research tools
  • Interactive Bible games with live friend challenges that reinforce biblical knowledge and Scripture learning
  • Accessible design for a wide range of users
  • Fast and intuitive navigation
  • Support for multiple Bible translations where licensing permits
  • A growing collection of features shaped by community feedback

As an educator, I've seen firsthand that learning is often most effective when it is interactive. That's why Big Screen Bible includes games and learning experiences designed to help individuals, families, classrooms, small groups, and churches deepen their knowledge of Scripture in engaging ways.

Does this help people engage more deeply with God's Word?

If the answer is yes, it belongs. If not, it probably doesn't.

Independent, personal, and shaped by ministry needs

A Personal Passion Project

Big Screen Bible is not backed by a large company, publishing organization, or development team.

It is currently a personal passion project that I develop and maintain independently, combining my interests in ministry, education, technology, and biblical study.

Every feature, improvement, and design decision is driven by a desire to create a resource that helps people interact with Scripture more effectively. As the project continues to grow, new ideas and enhancements are added as time, resources, and community feedback allow.

Designed to be useful without unnecessary barriers

Keeping It Accessible

One of the guiding principles behind Big Screen Bible is accessibility.

My hope is to keep this resource available to as many people as possible while continually improving its usefulness for churches, educators, worship leaders, students, families, and individual Bible readers around the world.

The goal has never been to create another complicated Bible platform. Instead, the vision is to provide a simple, powerful, and welcoming place where people can engage with God's Word without unnecessary barriers.

Where the project can grow from here

Looking Ahead

Big Screen Bible continues to evolve, but the vision remains the same.

I hope it becomes a trusted resource for:

  • Churches displaying Scripture during worship services
  • Teachers and Bible instructors
  • Small groups and ministry leaders
  • Families conducting worship at home
  • Students learning God's Word
  • Individuals seeking a distraction-free Bible experience
  • Anyone who wants Scripture to be more visible in their daily life

As the project grows, I look forward to expanding its features, improving accessibility, and discovering new ways to help people connect with the Bible.
